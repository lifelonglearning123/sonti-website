The short version

  • sonti.io sets no marketing cookies unless you allow them. Until you choose, nothing from TikTok loads.
  • The only optional tool is TikTok’s pixel, which shows us which of our TikTok ads bring agencies to the site.
  • You can change your choice at any time in cookie settings.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Local storage does a similar job, but only the website’s own code can read it. UK law treats both the same way, so this policy covers both.

The lifetimes below are what we measured on the live site on 12 September 2026. Cookies set by other companies can change without notice.

While the pixel is allowed, it tells TikTok which pages you view, when you open the booking calendar and when you send an enquiry. When you send the enquiry form, our server also reports it to TikTok with your email address in hashed form. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is sent.

Other services the site connects to

Like any website, these services receive your IP address when your browser contacts them. Apart from the __cf_bm cookie listed above, we found no cookies from them.

  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts on every page.
  • Our booking calendar loads only when you choose “Book a call”. It comes from link.knotie.ai, which runs on HighLevel, with files from leadconnectorhq.com and storage.googleapis.com. It also downloads a script from Meta (connect.facebook.net); when we checked on 12 September 2026, that script sent no tracking data and set no cookies.

Changing your choice

  • Use cookie settings to switch TikTok’s pixel on or off. Switching it off deletes TikTok’s cookies stored on sonti.io and our saved click ID.
  • TikTok’s own _ttp cookie on tiktok.com can only be deleted in your browser settings.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them, though the booking calendar may not.
