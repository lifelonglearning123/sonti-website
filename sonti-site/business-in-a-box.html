Questions new owners ask first

The honest answers, including what happens after the year. Anything else, ask us on the call.

Who counts as a new business?

Anyone who started trading in the last twelve months. For a limited company that's the date on your Companies House record. For a sole trader it's your first invoice or your first customer. We'll ask for one or the other on the call, and that's the only check.

What happens after the first year?

You choose. Keep all three at the normal monthly prices, which come to £248 a month for one person. Keep one or two. Or stop. We'll send you the year's numbers a month before, so you can decide on what actually happened. Nothing renews without you saying so.

Is there really nothing else to pay?

£250 plus VAT for the year, paid once. Signal includes 200 minutes of calls a month. If you get busier than that, extra minutes are billed at the rate on signal.macaws.ai. Another person in the Nexus inbox is £50 a month. That's the whole list.

Can I pay monthly instead?

No. It's one payment for the year, and that is how we can make it £250. If you'd rather pay monthly, the three tools are on the main site at their normal prices.

What if I close the business during the year?

Tell us and we switch everything off. There's no refund on the year, but there is nothing further to pay and no notice period.

How quickly can I be live?

Usually a week from the setup call. Signal is often answering the same afternoon. Nexus and the Google profile take a few days because we check them with you first.

Do I need a website?

No. Signal learns your business from what you tell us on the call, and your Google Business Profile becomes the page people find. If you do have a website, Signal reads it too.

Can I keep the number I've just got?

Yes. You point it at Signal, all the time or only when you can't answer, and you can point it back whenever you like. If you don't have a number yet, we give you one.
