The problem it was funded to solve

A receptionist that sounds human is no longer the hard part. Affording one is. The best voice technology is priced by the minute for call centres, so the bill grows with every call a business takes, which is exactly the wrong shape for a four-person practice, a builder or a boarding kennels. The project took that cost floor out: the same quality of conversation, at a price a small business can put on one monthly invoice without thinking about it.

  1. 01

    Voices from here, not a call centre

    The voices are trained to sound like someone in your town answering your phone, not an American help desk. Pauses, “mm-hmm”, a breath before a sentence.

  2. 02

    Understands how people actually talk

    Regional accents, a van engine in the background, the words a customer uses for a boiler fault. The system was trained on calls like yours, not clean studio recordings.

  3. 03

    Runs on machines we control

    The thinking happens on servers we run, so the price doesn't jump when a big tech company changes its prices, and your callers' details don't have to leave the UK to be understood.

  4. 04

    Answers within a beat

    Answer straight away and a caller talks normally. Answer a second late and they know something is off. Most of the engineering went into closing that gap.

That is the difference between a receptionist being something a call centre buys and something a plumber can.

Questions owners ask first

The ones that come up on nearly every first call. Anything else, ask us on the call itself.

Will callers know they're talking to a computer?

Usually not. Signal uses a natural voice with the pauses and “mm-hmm”s of a real receptionist, and most callers book the job and hang up without asking. If someone does ask, it tells them the truth, and it never pretends to be a named person. You can hear real calls on signal.macaws.ai before you decide.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and point it at Signal, either all the time or only when you can't answer, after hours and at weekends. We do that with you on the setup call, and you can switch it back whenever you like. If you'd rather have a new number just for Signal, that works too.

What happens when it can't answer something?

It says so, takes the caller's name, number and what they wanted, tells them when you'll call back, and emails you the written-up call. If a caller needs you now, it puts them straight through to your mobile. It never makes up a price or a promise it hasn't been given.

How long does it take to set up?

Signal is usually answering within a day of the setup call, often the same afternoon. Nexus and the Google profile fixes take a few days, because we check them with you before they go live. A week from first call to everything running is typical.

Do I need to learn any software?

No. We set everything up and you get Nexus on your phone, which works like a messaging app. Everything else, the answering, the reminders, the Google posts, happens on its own. When you want something changed, you message us and we change it.

Where does my customers' information go?

It stays on servers in the UK and EU, under UK data protection law. Calls are recorded with a short announcement, as the law requires, and the recordings and messages are yours. We never sell or share customer details, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Everything is monthly with no long contract and no setup fee. Give us 30 days' notice and your last month runs to the end. Your customer list and your call records come with you.

What's in the free Google audit?

Six things: how complete your profile is, your reviews, how many you've replied to, your categories and services, your photos, and your posts and questions. You get a score out of 100 and a short list of fixes, in plain English, with nothing to pay and no obligation to go further.

What does “a seat” mean for Nexus?

One person logged in. If it's just you, you need one seat. If a receptionist or a partner should see the inbox too, add a seat for each of them. Seats can be added or removed month to month.

Does it work with my diary, my website and the tools I already use?

Almost always. Signal books into Google Calendar, Outlook and the calendar in Nexus. Nexus connects to WhatsApp, email, your website forms and thousands of other apps. If you use something unusual, tell us on the call and we'll check before you pay anything.
